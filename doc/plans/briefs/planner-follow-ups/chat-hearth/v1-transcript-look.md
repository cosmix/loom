## Fixed targets (what the shots must show, Walnut and Ledger)

1. **User messages** (`items.tsx` `UserItem`, `chat-transcript.css`): a bubble on the right, `max-width: 86%`, `--muted` fill, corners 14px 14px 4px 14px, padding 10px 14px, 15px/1.5 sans, `--foreground`. A quoted passage sits inside the bubble above the words; sent chips sit under the bubble, right-aligned. Copy and time appear on hover or focus under the bubble, as now.
2. **Assistant replies** (`TextItem`): on the left at the reply's full width (no 30px avatar column), `--font-reading` 16px/1.55, paragraphs spaced 0.75em, links as reading prose.
3. **An offer's opening** (the `origin === "offer"` item): one quiet centred line in `--muted-foreground` at `--type-ui`: its label and the Show link; shown, the prompt opens under it as muted pre-wrapped text. It is never a bubble. Keep the frozen DOM: no prompt text in the DOM until Show, Show's `aria-controls` (chat-fixes contract).
4. **Working card** (`transcript.tsx` `FoldedTools`, `tool-row.tsx`): the fold is a card, `--background` on the `--card` panel, corner 14px, padding 12px 16px, no border. Its summary line keeps the frozen label ("Used 3 tools · edited 2 files") in 13px/500 `--muted-foreground`; while the turn is open the card is open. Each tool row is one line: an 18px column with a 14px lucide glyph by tool (`FileText` Read, `Pencil` Edit/Write/MultiEdit, `Terminal` Bash, `Search` Grep/Glob, `Bot` Agent, `Wrench` otherwise), the target in `--font-mono` `--type-ui` `--foreground` cut by an ellipsis, and at the end the counts in mono `--type-fine` (`+N` in `--tone-completed`, `−M` in `--destructive`) or the status word. A pending row's target is `--accent-ink` and a soft shimmer sweeps across the row (a 100deg linear gradient of `--primary` at 10% through `color-mix`, `background-size: 240% 100%`, 1.8s linear, infinite). Rows stay buttons that open their details (`aria-expanded`, Input / Patch / Result as now). Error and denied rows stay outside the card with their note.
5. **Turn activity** (`.chat-turn-activity`): the 16px roundel and, after an 8px gap, nothing visible (the sr-only word stays), sitting at the end of the working turn at the reply's left edge.
6. **Permission card** (`permission-card.tsx`, `chat-decision.css`): a grid of `HazardRail` (tone warning) and the body; the card is `--background`, corner 14px, overflow hidden, 1px border of the hazard warning at 25% (`color-mix`), no shadow. Title row 15px/600 with lucide `TriangleAlert` (16px, `--tone-warning`) and the tool question; body text 15px in `--font-reading`; inline code mono 13px on `--muted`, corner 6px. Actions: Allow as the card's primary pill, Deny as a secondary pill (V2 adds `shape="pill"` to `DisabledReason`; if it is not there yet, report it). The names stay "Allow" and "Deny" (frozen `chat.contracts`), as do `role="region"`, `aria-label="Permission: <tool>"`, `.chat-decision-actions` and its first and last child (help pins), the `y`/`n` keys, `DenyNoteForm`, "Waiting for <holder> to decide" and the single `⚠`-free card (the help scene counts one `⚠`, in the state line).
7. **Wrap-up card** (`.chat-wrap`, state 4): `--background`, corner 14px, padding 16px, gap 12px. Head: the roundel centred in a 36px `--muted` circle, then the title 15px/600 and the line under it in `--type-ui` `--muted-foreground`. Ticks: 20px circles with a 1.5px `--border` edge; done fills `--tone-completed` with a `--card` check drawn by a stroke-dashoffset animation (500ms, 300ms delay); live has a `--primary` edge; paths mono `--type-ui` cut by an ellipsis.
8. **Left-behind card** (`.chat-left-behind`, state 5): `--background`, corner 14px, padding 16px, gap 12px. Title in `--font-reading` 20px/500, letter-spacing -0.01em. Counts as neutral pills 26px high on `--muted` (13px/500, the number at 600; `✓` in `--tone-completed`, `?` in `--tone-warning`). Documents: an 8px dot, the path mono `--type-ui` cut by an ellipsis, the counts as in the working card. "Decided" and "Still open" `h4` at `--type-ui`/600. Decisions: an `ol` in `--font-reading` 15px/1.45, each led by its number in a 20px `--muted` disc (CSS counter). Questions: italic `--font-reading` 15px, each led by a `?` in a 20px disc of the hazard warning at 18%. "See the changes": a quiet pill link.
9. **Starting card** (`.chat-starting`): the roundel, "Starting the session" in `--type-body` `--foreground`, the brief line under it in `--type-ui` muted.
10. **Breaks**: the session divider (`session-divider.tsx`) is a centred `--type-ui` muted line between two hairlines; a notice keeps its ruled break; an API error keeps the exact rule `transcript.test.tsx` matches (`.chat-notice-item[data-kind="api_error"] .chat-notice-glyph { color: var(--tone-blocked);`).
11. **Turn footer** (`turn-footer.tsx`): the changed files as quiet chips on one wrapping row under the turn, Diff and Restore as quiet `sm` buttons; the group name "Turn N changes" and its links stay (frozen `chat.contracts`, help pin).
12. **Rhythm and motion**: the transcript is a column with a 16px gap and the 18px `--chat-gutter`. A new message, card or row enters with a rise (8px up, scale 0.985 to 1, 460ms `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), staggered 70ms for items arriving together. Every animation (rise, shimmer, drawn checks; the roundel's spin is BusyRoundel's own) is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and the drawn checks show complete.
13. At 390px (the chat sheet) nothing scrolls sideways; long paths cut, bubbles wrap.
