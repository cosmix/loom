# Planner visual system

Rules for every planner surface. The scales and primitives are in `design.css` (imported once from `plan.css`); the tone comes from the reading column on the product page: type and space do the work, and the chrome stays out of the way of the text. A rule that fails in one of the three themes (Ledger, Walnut, Graphite) fails here.

## Colour

- Theme tokens only: `--background`, `--card`, `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--muted-foreground`, `--border`, `--hairline`, `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--ring`, `--accent-ink`, `--accent-text`, `--tone-*`, `--doc-1..8`. No colour literal, no `color-mix` that invents a hue, no gradient.
- The themes (`tokens.css`): Ledger, light, stone paper with the amber `--primary` `oklch(0.84 0.155 80)`; Walnut, dark (offered in the dashboard and the planner), a warm brown-black at hue 55 (`.dark`); Graphite, the dark default (`theme-default.ts` seeds it), a neutral charcoal at hue 270 (`.dark[data-dark-theme="gray"]`); Aubergine, a purple dark at hue 325 (`.dark[data-dark-theme="aubergine"]`), is dashboard-only and not offered in the planner. Dark surfaces (`--background`, `--card`, `--muted`, `--secondary`, `--accent`, `--border`, the sidebar tokens) keep chroma at or under 0.015, so the only saturated colours on a page are the accent and the status tones. Measured (WCAG relative luminance from OKLCH): Walnut's foreground on its background 14.6:1, muted foreground on its card 6.7:1, the orange on its background 7.7:1 and the background on the orange 7.7:1; Graphite 14.7:1, 6.6:1, 7.5:1 and 7.5:1.
- The dark accent is orange, `oklch(0.74 0.15 55)`, for `--primary` and `--ring`, with `--primary-foreground` the theme's own background. The accent marks the primary button's fill, the focus ring, the current spine row's mark and dot, the selection and the arrival flash in the reading column, and the chosen option of a segmented choice, and nothing else. It is never a border, a tint behind a surface, a heading colour or a status.
- Contrast (WCAG relative luminance from the OKLCH tokens): text meets 4.5:1 (1.4.3) and a control's edge, a focus ring or a glyph that carries meaning meets 3:1 (1.4.11) against what it sits on, in every theme. `--accent-ink` (`index.css`: `--ring` on Ledger, `--primary` in the dark themes) is the token for accent-coloured lines and glyphs (the specimen's rule, the chosen size tile's edge and glyph, the live wrap-up dot), 3.5:1 on the Ledger page and 3.8:1 on its card, where the amber fill is 1.5:1. `--accent-text` (`index.css`: a deeper ochre `oklch(0.5 0.11 80)` on Ledger, `--primary` in the dark themes) is the token for an accent-coloured text run (New work's eyebrow, a pending tool row's target), 4.9:1 on the Ledger page, 5.3:1 on its card and 4.6:1 on `--muted`; in the dark themes both are the orange at 7.5:1 or more.
- In the dark themes the warning tones (`--tone-warning`, `--hazard-warning`) are amber at hue 80, 25 degrees off the orange accent at hue 55, so a warning never reads as the accent; Ledger's warning stays orange (hue 50) beside its amber accent. Every dark tone measures at least 3.4:1 on the Walnut and the Graphite card (`--tone-completed` 8.8:1 and 8.6:1), and the Ledger tones at least 3.4:1 on the Ledger card. As every status, a warning is a glyph and a word, never a hue alone.
- `--tone-*` colours a glyph, never a text run, a border or a background. Warnings use `--tone-warning`; `--destructive` is for an action that deletes.

## Type

Chrome is Inter (`--font-sans`); reading prose keeps `--font-reading`; paths, ids and code keep `--font-mono`. Sentence case everywhere; the `eyebrow` utility is the only uppercase.

| Token          | Size                            | Use                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `--type-page`  | 20px/650, `.plan-page-title`    | the one title of a screen, under its eyebrow                                                                        |
| `--type-title` | 15px/600, `.plan-section-title` | a section inside a page, a panel head                                                                               |
| `--type-body`  | 14px/400                        | chrome prose: a sentence beside a button, a note, an empty state; the spine's rows and the chat's rows and controls |
| `--type-ui`    | 13px/500                        | buttons, rows, tabs, labels; in the spine and the chat, what is `.plan-fine` elsewhere                              |
| `--type-fine`  | 12px/400, `.plan-fine`          | a reason under a control, a count, a timestamp                                                                      |

The two sidebars sit one step up the scale from the rest of the chrome, because they are read beside the reading column, not glanced at. In the spine (`feature/spine.css`): rows and the group names at `--type-body`; the mono ids and numbers, the counts, a section's state word and the "Source" line at `--type-ui`; a plan row's state and pressure record are 14px glyphs. In the chat panel (`chat.css` root is `--type-body`): chrome (tool rows and fold line, notices, chip labels, context row, queue rows, permission band) at `--type-body`; the head title at `--type-title` (600; "New chat" at 500 in `--muted-foreground`) over its sub-line, the state word in `--foreground` and the place in `--muted-foreground`, at `--type-ui`; the empty state's title at 26px/500 and the left-behind title at 20px/500, both in `--font-reading`; user turns and the composer field at 15px (`--type-title`, turns at 500, field at 400); the assistant's prose at 16px/1.55 in `--font-reading`; secondary text (chip source, file size, timestamps, counts, `.plan-fine`, `size="sm"` buttons) and mono tool and permission bodies at `--type-ui`; the composer's foot line at `--type-fine`, the panel's one 12px text (Chat). The kickoff transcript inherits the chat's sizes. Full-size buttons in the chat are 14px; the hearth's shaped buttons (Chat) are 32px. Nothing else in either sidebar drops below 13px.

Hierarchy is made with size, weight and space, in that order. A surface uses the weights the scale above names (400, 500, 600, and 650 for the page title) and no others. Muted text (`--muted-foreground`) marks what is secondary; it never marks what is disabled.

## Space

`--space-1..7` = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px. Inside a control and between a glyph and its word: 1-2. Between rows: 2-3. Between a title and its content: 3. Between sections: 5-6, with no rule and no box between them. Page gutter: `--page-gutter` (24px, 16px below 480px). Reading text keeps its 68ch column; chrome prose stops at 48ch.

## Surfaces and borders

- The page ground is `--background`. One level of surface exists: `.plan-surface` (`--card`, hairline edge, 10px corners) standing on the ground. Nothing inside a surface draws a border, a shadow or another surface. A box in a box is not allowed.
- A border is allowed around: an input, a surface standing alone on the page, a popover or dialog. A border is not allowed around: a button row, a list that sits on the page, a status, a heading, a notice.
- Lists divide with hairlines (`.plan-list`, `.plan-rule`); their edges are the page's edges. A hairline separates; it never encloses.
- Shadows exist only on things that float: popovers and dialogs (`--plan-shadow`, `--dialog-shadow`; the activity popover layers `--popover-lift`, a longer drop, under `--plan-shadow`). Nothing in the page flow casts one.
- An open block in the reading column (a sentence opened to its source passages) draws a 2px edge down its start in its document's hue (`--doc-n`, through `--run-colour`). It is the one coloured border on the page: the hue says which document, never a status.
- The chat composer is a bordered input inside the chat panel, so the place to type stands apart from the transcript around it; its 22px corners and the tools inside it are the hearth's (Chat).
- exception: the document heading's toolbar (`.doc-heading-toolbar`, `doc-heading.css`; Page rhythm) is one `--border`-edged bar with control corners around every control over the document, its groups divided by 1px rules of the same edge. The controls inside are all quiet row controls, so without the bar they read as loose words; the bar is what says they are one instrument. The Summary / Source switch (`.doc-heading-views`) is the bar's first group, two flush segments with no track of their own, the current view filled in `--muted` and set in `--foreground` at 600: a two-state control has to show its state at rest in every theme, and a pair of quiet buttons cannot.
- exception: the document heading's status card (`.doc-heading-status`, a `.plan-surface` of rows divided by hairlines) holds a quiet button at a row's end (Rebuild, Rebuild all): the card is a status list, the action belongs to its row, and a quiet button draws no second edge inside the surface.
- exception: the document key under an overview's head (`document-key.tsx`, `concept-page.css`; a summary page draws none) sets each document on a fixed-width grid track (mark, mono number, title cut by `text-overflow: ellipsis`), with the title in `--foreground` among muted chrome. Without hovering, the title is the only thing that says which document a colour is; the track keeps a long key in columns.
- exception: on a feature document's source route the spine's current row carries a `--type-fine` "Source" line under its name, drawn by CSS (`spine.css`, `data-view="source"`), not a DOM node: the heading's switch already names the view to assistive technology.
- exception: a plan row in the spine's Plans group (`spine.css`, `.plan-spine__glyph`, drawn by `feature/spine-row.tsx`) shows its state and its pressure record as 14px glyphs alone after the mono slug, on one line: the slug has the width the two glyphs leave and is cut by an end ellipsis only when longer than that. Slugs run to about thirty characters and the state and record as words to about 16rem at 13px, more than the 18rem spine holds beside a slug. Each glyph's word stays in the link in `.sr-only`, so the link's name and text read "slug done Pressure-tested <date>", and the glyph's wrapper (hidden from assistive technology) carries the same word as its `title`, the hover tooltip; a retired plan shows its state glyph and no record.
- exception: the Size row in Taking shape (`new/taking-shape.css`, `.kickoff-seg`, drawn by `new/shape-size.tsx`) is a hairline-edged track around its two segments, the chosen one filled in `--muted` and set in `--foreground` at 600, as the document heading's switch is: a two-state control has to show its state at rest in every theme. Kind of work on the empty form is the pill track under New work.
- exception: in the live kickoff conversation (`new/kickoff.css`, `.kickoff-transcript`) the user's turns are set at 15px/400 (`--type-title` size at body weight) and the assistant's prose at `--reading-size` on its 68ch measure, because the kickoff is read as a document, so its type is the reading column's, not the chat panel's. Its head is the Hearth chat head (`.kickoff-head`: the title at `--type-title`/600, the panel head's state words at `--type-ui`, idle saying nothing) over the page's one hairline, with no eyebrow and no busy sign: the transcript's starting card carries the pane's one spinner, and a working turn its typing bubble.
- exception: inside Taking shape (`new/taking-shape.css`) `.plan-fine` text (a reason under a control, a status) is set at `--type-ui`, and the name and section numbers are mono at `--type-ui`: the panel keeps the 13px floor of panel text.

It is the one gradient between two surface colours in the planner: the two headers belong to one product and have to read as one. ## Buttons

`DisabledReason` draws every button (`.plan-button`, 28px high, 13px/500, 6px corners, one line, `white-space: nowrap`). `OfferButton` wraps it for an offer. Three looks through `look`:

- `quiet`: no edge, muted text until hovered. Toolbar actions, Source and Edit, icon buttons.
- exception: the header's Chat button (`ChatSheetButton`, `chat/panel-collapse.tsx`) is a quiet `.plan-button` that is always shown on a chat route. From 1280px it is the side panel's disclosure: `aria-expanded` while the panel is open fills it `--muted` in `--foreground` (the `.plan-button` expanded state), and it is named "Collapse chat" or "Expand chat" with the state and counts. Below 1280px it opens the sheet. Its decision count is a 16px `--primary` pill and its attachment count a 16px `--muted` pill with a `--border` ring, both inside the 28px line box.
- `secondary`: hairline edge, transparent. Every offer and action by default.
- `primary`: `--primary` fill. At most one per screen, and only its owner passes `look="primary"`: the surface that draws `checklist.primary` (the pace card holding it on `product` and `engineering` while pace shows, the queue row, or the page that owns it), a dialog's confirm (a dialog floats and owns its own), and the Start of the home and kickoff screens. `DisabledReason` is secondary unless told otherwise. The chat's own primaries, one per surface, are under Chat.

`shape` (`pill` or `round`, drawn as `data-shape`, `ui/hearth.css`) is the hearth's 32px full-radius variant, used only in the chat and on `/new` (Chat); it wins over `size="sm"`. `reasonId` names an element the caller draws the blocked reason in; the button is described by it and draws none of its own, so the reason is still drawn once.

`size="sm"` is the 24px/12px row variant for an action inside a list row, a panel head or the document heading (Page rhythm), where every control (Edit, Discuss, Open all and Mark settled in the toolbar bar, the Summary / Source switch's segments, Rebuild at a status row's end) is a bare `.plan-button` of that size, quiet, on one 24px centre line. The heading draws no secondary and no primary button: the bar and the status card carry the edges, and the quiet buttons sit inside them. A label is a verb plus at most two words (`Start`, `Move`, `Rebuild`), except that an offer's server label is drawn as sent (`Write plan feat-planner-01-documents`); a label carrying a document's slug truncates and never wraps. The sentence beside the button explains, so the button never carries the explanation. A screen never draws one offer twice: each offer has one owner, decided by its id.

Blocked: `aria-disabled`, 50% opacity, no hover change, and the reason beneath in `.plan-fine`, or after the button on its line in the document header's summary status line (`reasonPlacement="inline"`). The reason is the control's accessible description, so it is drawn once per blocked control and never repeated in a second line or a banner under the same control.

## Status

Status is a glyph and a word (`.plan-status`), inline with the text it describes: `✓ done`, `○ not written`, `· optional`, and the current item marked with `--primary`. The word keeps the text's colour; only the glyph takes a tone. No coloured chips, no pill backgrounds, no segmented progress bars, no coloured left borders for a status (an open block's edge, under Surfaces, names a document). A count reads as text (`12/12`, `3 plans`) in `.plan-fine`. An out-of-date item is dashed, not recoloured. The document key writes `changed` in `.plan-fine` beside a document that changed since the reader looked.

Five signs, one per job, and none stands in for another:

1. **The roundel** (`BusyRoundel`, `@/aurora-ui/feedback/BusyRoundel`), the black-and-white disc, is the header notification pill's alone (`header/activity.tsx`, `PillGlyph`): the planner's global activity indicator, turning while any work runs. It is drawn nowhere else in the planner, inside an `aria-hidden` wrapper sized by `.activity-glyph` (the disc at 86% of the box, the reach of a lucide glyph's strokes). When the most urgent activity is your turn, the pill draws that kind's bell or LED instead.
2. **A state word** ("Running", "Drafting", "Rebuilding", "Working", "In progress", "Session starting") is led by the two rotating arrows (`ArrowSpinner`, `ui/spinner.tsx`, drawn as lucide's two-arrow `RefreshCw`; a single-arrow icon never stands in for it): in `currentColor`, turning once per 1.1s, in the 14px glyph box by default (1em in `BusyText`), still under `prefers-reduced-motion` and dimmed when `busy={false}`. `StateMark` (`ui/activity-glyphs.tsx`) is the arrows for an activity kind, `ActivityTile` draws it for the state-word kinds (`STATE_WORD_KINDS`), `PLAN_STATE_WORD_GLYPHS.running` is the plan state's, and `BusyText` (`ui/busy-text.tsx`) is the arrows then the busy words ("Sending…") inside the caller's own role. `SUMMARY_STATE_GLYPHS.rebuilding`, the document mark of a rebuilding summary (`ui/doc-mark.tsx`), the Chat button while a session starts (`chat/chat-state-glyph.tsx`), a drafting teammate or queue row and a setting being saved are the arrows.
3. **The item or action itself** (a plan, a document, a pressure test, a Codex review, a chat) is led by the status dot: the LED of `ActivityGlyph` (`ui/activity-glyphs.tsx`, `.activity-glyph__led`), one pattern for every kind but permission (the bell). Live is `--tone-completed` and pulses gently (the 2s `activity-glyph-waiting` cycle), still under `prefers-reduced-motion`; paused (`paused`) is `--muted-foreground` and still. `PLAN_STATE_GLYPHS.running` (`LiveDot`) is the same dot before a plan. The rule is by what follows the mark: a state word gets the arrows, the item or action gets the dot, so a plan row led by its name has the dot and the "Running" chip has the arrows. A dot is always beside its word, or the word is the glyph's label: never hue alone. A pressure run's steps in the chat take the arrows, one per running step, even with steps side by side; a finished step shows its done check, a step not yet started the dashed circle. A gray dot never stands for a running step.
4. **A block-sized loading, empty or error state** is `LoadState` with the larger `Spinner` or a quiet icon, centred (Loading, empty and error states). The chat's starting and wrap-up cards draw the 20px `Spinner` too.
5. **Counted work** is a progress bar that carries the count: the pressure run's row in the activity popover (`header/activity-rows.tsx`) keeps its determinate bar, with the plan, round, step and elapsed time; no strip sits under the app bar.

The roundel (sign 1) is the one exception to the arrows-before-state-words rule: it marks only the header's activity pill. `RefreshCw` also remains the icon of a button whose action is "Rebuild". The pressure dialog's hero mark is the still pressure-tested flask: a dialog's mark is not work in progress.

A document's settle state (`trace/trace.css`, `[data-settle]`) has three tones and no fourth: `draft` is `--muted-foreground` (an outline circle in the spine, an outline dot in the pill), `settled` is `--tone-completed`, and the three moved states `revised`, `stub_changed` and `upstream_changed` share `--tone-warning`, so one amber mark means "look again" wherever it appears; the word beside it (`settleLabel`) says which. In the spine the mark is one more 14px glyph after the row's name (`SettleMark`, `feature/spine-row.tsx`), its word in `.sr-only` and the tooltip as the pressure record's is, so the slug keeps the row's width. The turn footer's impact rows (`.trace-impact`) and a plan page's spec chips (`.trace-chip-state`) draw the state as a 6px dot in the tone before its word. Done work is never asked to be settled: a document the trace marks `settleable: false` (a stub, a running, done, retired or archived plan, and the vision and metaplan of a feature whose plans are all done) draws no settle mark, pill, control, stub note, coverage flag or impact state anywhere (`shownSettle`, `trace/model.ts`), and the server gives it no settle gap. A done feature's product sections stay settleable, because a follow-up plan can still change what they promise.

A finding's status (`trace/maps-findings.css`, `.maps-finding-status`, read from `data-status` on its card) is the settle state's pill with the same rule, a 7px dot in the tone before the word: `open` takes `--tone-warning`, the one amber that says "needs you" wherever it appears on the trace pages; `planned` and `folded` are quiet, a `--muted-foreground` dot with the word in `--foreground`, because a plan or an entry has taken them up; `dismissed` is an outline dot with the word, the title and the text in `--muted-foreground`, the one status read as past. On the coverage map an open finding's card carries the same amber dot and the word "Open" under its title, and a heading nothing covers a dashed ring and "Not covered" in muted ink: a gap is an absence, not a warning.

- exception: the document heading's settle state (`.trace-settle-state`, drawn by `trace/settle-control.tsx`) is a 24px `--muted` pill, the chat file chip's shape, set in `--foreground` at 500 with its 7px dot in the tone: it leads the toolbar bar's second group (Page rhythm), after the Summary / Source switch, where a bare word read as a stray label; its pill shape is what tells a state from the quiet actions beside it. Mark settled and Unsettle are quiet `sm` buttons after it in the same group, and Changes since settled is a link at that size; a note after them is one `--type-ui` line in `--foreground` led by a dot (`--tone-blocked` for an alert, muted for a status).
- exception: the stub panel (`.trace-stub`) is the reading face at 0.85 of `--reading-size` in `--muted-foreground`, its title in `--foreground` at 500: the words that set a document's scope, read before the body and quieter than it. In the document heading (`doc-heading.css`) it is a `--muted` wash with the surface corners and no edge, its place ("Stub in the vision") a `--type-fine` muted label over the words, the one filled block in the heading, so a quotation reads apart from the status card's edged rows; on the source route (`.trace-source-head`) it keeps a 2px `--hairline` on its start edge. "Stub changed since settled" under it is a `--type-ui` line led by a `--tone-warning` dot. The stub holds only the stub.
- exception: a plan's briefs (`trace/briefs-card.tsx`, `briefs.css`) are a card of their own under the stub, a `.plan-surface` so it reads apart from the stub's wash: a head row (a muted 14px files glyph, "Briefs" at `--type-ui`/600, the count at `--type-fine` at its end), then one group per folder under the plan's `briefs/<slug>/`, the top-level files first, each folder in mono `--type-fine` muted with its count, over its files as 24px `--muted` mono chips at `--type-fine` showing the file's stem (the link's name keeps `folder/file.md`), in natural order. A bundle of more than six files starts folded: the count becomes a quiet `sm` disclosure ("25 briefs" with a turning chevron) and one mono `--type-fine` line names the folders and their counts. A list of thirty paths read as noise in the stub's quotation; grouped chips let the reader find a stage's briefs by its folder.

A plan row's pressure record (`.plan-spine__badge`, `feature/spine.css`) is a glyph alone, beside the plan's state glyph (the exception under Surfaces and borders): a flask in `--tone-completed` for a tested plan, a gauge in `--muted-foreground` for an untested one. Its words, "Pressure-tested <date>" or "Not pressure-tested", are the same as the plan page's chip; they are read from `.sr-only` and shown as the glyph's tooltip. A retired plan has no record. The state glyph beside it (`data-state`, `feature/spine-row.tsx`) takes `--tone-completed` when the plan is done, as the tested flask does; the written pencil, the retired glyph and the untested gauge stay `--muted-foreground`, and a running plan's live dot is green and pulses (Status), so a finished, tested plan reads as two green marks, a running one as a live dot, and everything else as quiet chrome.

## Chat

The chat panel is the hearth (`chat/chat-surface.css`, `chat-composer.css`, `chat-transcript.css`, `ui/hearth.css`): the warm end of the page, where the rules above bend in these ways and no others.

- The user's messages are bubbles on the right in `--muted`; the assistant's replies run loose on the left in the reading face; no avatar is drawn beside either. No busy sign is an avatar (Status), so a reply has nothing to sit beside, and the words alone say who is speaking.
- The hearth glow (`.chat-panel::after`) is `--primary` at 14% (`color-mix`) fading out from the panel's foot, behind the composer, in every state. It and New work's wash (New work) are the planner's two accent tints under a surface (Colour): each marks a place the product talks back, and the glow says where.
- Pill and round buttons (`DisabledReason shape`, `data-shape`) exist only in the chat and on `/new`: 32px, full radius, pressed to 0.98. The chat draws more than one primary, one per surface: Send in the composer box, Allow on the permission card, Start a new chat on the ended line. Each surface has one action the user came to it for.
- The composer is one round bordered box (`.chat-composer-box`, 22px corners, `--background`) holding the context row, the pending chips, the borderless text and, on the text's last line, the tools: the paperclip (quiet, round), Stop (secondary, round, a filled square) and Send (primary, round, an arrow). A blocked Send's reason is the foot line under the box (`.chat-composer-foot`, `DisabledReason reasonId`), which otherwise reads "Enter to send" or "Your message waits for the turn to finish"; the composer's notes follow it there. The foot is `--type-fine`, the panel's one 12px text: a hint under a field, not chrome to be read.
- The empty state is a greeting in the reading face at 26px/500 over one muted line, bottom-aligned above the suggestion pills (`.hearth-pill`: 30px, hairline edge, lifting 1px under the pointer) and the box. The pills are the prompts for the page being read; with none, the row is absent and nothing moves.
- The ending and ended lines (`.chat-composer-ending`, `.chat-composer-ended`) are italic reading face at 15px in `--muted-foreground`; ended, Start a new chat follows as the primary pill over the glow.
- The working card's shimmer, the typing bubble's dots and the wrap-up card's drawn checks are the chat's three decorative motions, each tied to work in progress; they draw nothing when nothing works.
- A working chat turn ends with a typing bubble (`.chat-typing`, `chat-transcript.css`): the user's bubble mirrored to the left, `--muted` with its square corner at the bottom left, holding three 6px `--muted-foreground` dots that rise 3px and brighten in turn (1.2s, 160ms apart) and rest at 0.6 under `prefers-reduced-motion`; its sr-only words say "Working". It is a waiting sign, not a typing effect: replies still arrive a block at a time.
- One live sign per working thing in a pane (a pressure run's steps running side by side turn one arrow each), and the roundel is the header pill's alone: the starting card and the wrap-up card (while the closing write runs) draw the 20px `Spinner`, a pressure run's running step its turning arrow (Codex's card rests beside its row's arrow), and when nothing works nothing moves; a working chat turn draws the typing bubble instead. The head draws no busy sign and no state glyph but the permission warning; its sub-line is the state's words, then the place the chat concerns.
- The chat's cards (`chat-tools.css`, `chat-cards.css`, `chat-decision.css`: the working card, wrap-up, left-behind, permission) are `--background` surfaces on the `--card` panel with a 14px corner (`--chat-card-corner`) and no border, except the permission card's 1px edge in `--hazard-warning` at 25% beside aurora-ui's `HazardRail`: the decision is the one thing allowed real emphasis.
- A pending tool row (`chat-tools.css`, `[data-status="pending"]::after`) sets its target in `--accent-text` and sweeps a 100deg gradient of `--primary` at 10% through `color-mix`, 1.8s linear: a transparency of the accent that says "working now", never a hue of its own.
- The wrap-up's ticks (`chat-cards.css`) are 20px circles on the `--border` edge: the done one fills with `--tone-completed` and draws a `--card` check by `stroke-dashoffset` (500ms, 300ms delay); the live one holds an 8px `--accent-ink` dot inside the same edge, so it differs from a waiting tick by shape and not by hue alone, and the accent stays off the border; the next ones wait empty. The left-behind card's count pills sit on `--muted` with only their glyph toned; its decisions are numbered in 20px `--muted` discs and its open questions are led by a `?` in a disc of `--hazard-warning` at 18%.
- A tool row's and the left-behind card's `+N −M` counts (`.chat-counts`) are mono at `--type-fine` (12px, under the sidebar's 13px floor), `+N` in `--tone-completed` and `−M` in `--tone-blocked`, the one toned text: a figure beside a path, read at 4.5:1 in every theme (Ledger 4.8:1 and 4.9:1 on the page).
- The turn footer's changed files (`chat-decision.css`, `.chat-file-chip`) are 24px `--muted` pills in mono, and Diff is a link drawn as a quiet `sm` `.plan-button` (`.chat-footer-action`).
- The panel keeps one hairline, its start edge: no rule under the head and none over the composer. The empty state and the composer enter with the rise (`hearth-rise`, `chat-rise`: 8px, 460ms, `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`). Every motion in the chat is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Page rhythm

Eyebrow, page title, `--space-5`, then content. Sections follow at `--space-6` with a `.plan-section-title`. Rows sit on hairlines at 36px minimum height. The chrome never competes with the reading column: the spine and the chat panel are set in `--muted-foreground`, with the current item in `--foreground`, at the sidebar sizes under Type (rows at `--type-body`, their counts, ids and notes at `--type-ui`; the chat's chrome at `--type-body`, its prose at 16px). The spine keeps its 18rem column at that size; a plan row's state and pressure record are glyphs there, so its slug has the row's width. At 390px nothing scrolls sideways. Chrome stays at `--type-ui` or larger, except `--type-fine` text and the mono ids, numbers and paths set at that size outside the two sidebars.

Map pages (`trace/maps.css`, which imports `trace/maps-columns.css` first so its width steps cascade last; `trace/maps-findings.css`). The coverage page (`trace/coverage-page.tsx`) is a map, not a reading column: its `.plan-page` drops the 68ch cap (`:has(> .maps-coverage)`) and lays out by its own width through a container query on `.maps-coverage`, because the page's width beside the spine and the chat, not the viewport's, decides what fits. Five columns under eyebrow titles, divided by hairlines and joined by no lines, each a list of compact cards in the planner's card style (`--card`, hairline edge, 8px corners between a control's and a surface's, the muted wash on hover) at `--type-ui`/500; the spec column groups its headings under their section's name at `--type-ui`/500 in muted ink. A card's flag is a glyph and a word under its title (Status): a gap is a dashed card with a dashed ring and "Not covered", a moved document or an open finding a 7px `--tone-warning` dot and its word, drawn with empty alternative text because the card's `.sr-only` description already says it. Lineage is the accent: the selected card fills `--muted` at 600 and it and its relatives carry a 6px `--accent-ink` dot in the column's gutter, as the spine marks its current row; the cards outside the lineage lose their fill, fade their edge and set in muted ink at 400, legible still. The filter is a segmented choice: a hairline pill track around native radios, the chosen segment filled `--muted` at 600, and under 40rem single pills that wrap. The focus panel (`aside.maps-focus`) docks on the right from 56rem of page width, 16rem behind a hairline on its start edge and stuck to the top of the scrollport; below that it is a sheet stuck to the foot of the scrollport behind a hairline on the page ground, its three lists side by side from 40rem, with no shadow because it is in the page flow. Under 40rem the columns stack as a list of sections, each with its layers in order, so nothing scrolls sideways. The findings page (`trace/findings-page.tsx`) keeps the reading width: groups under eyebrows at `--space-6`, each finding one planner card holding the mono id, the title at `--type-title`/600 with the status pill at the line's end, the text in the reading face at 0.85 of `--reading-size`, the About chips (the plan page's `.trace-chip`) led by the word, and the actions as quiet buttons; the fold picker and the reason field open in place under them, the field the card's one bordered thing. The pill caps at 28rem so a long dismissal reason wraps inside it, and under 36rem of card width (a container query on the card) it drops under the head. A card addressed by `#finding-<id>` takes the `--muted` wash.

The document heading (`ui/doc-heading.tsx`, `doc-heading.css`; a summary page's through `concept/page-heading.tsx`) is one column of blocks on the column's left edge, read top to bottom, over the hairline that ends it: the breadcrumb (`.plan-fine`, `--space-2` over the title); the title row, the 30px document mark and the title on one line band; `--space-4`, then the toolbar bar; then `--space-3` between each of the status card, the facts line, the stub and a plan's own rows. The toolbar bar (Surfaces) is its own row under the title, never beside it: a 32px `--border`-edged track whose three groups (`ToolbarGroup`, `.doc-heading-group`) are divided by rules of the same edge, every control inside a 24px row control on one centre line: the Summary / Source switch; the settle state pill with Mark settled or Unsettle; Edit, Discuss and Open all. A group that renders nothing collapses (`:empty`) and its rule goes with it; under 40rem of heading width the bar stacks its groups as rows divided by hairlines, so nothing wraps mid group and nothing scrolls sideways. The status card (`.doc-heading-status`, `StatusCard`, a `.plan-surface`) is what needs attention, one 36px row per status divided by hairlines: a toned 14px glyph, the words in `--foreground` at 500, a `.plan-fine` detail line under the words on their left edge, and the row's one action as a quiet `sm` button at its end (under 30rem it moves under the words). The summary's state leads, with Rebuild or Rebuild all and the pending rebuild's sentence as its detail; then the open questions (a link row); then a plan's state and pressure record. The facts line (`.doc-heading-facts`, `FactsLine`, a `dl`) follows the card as quiet label and value pairs at `--type-ui`, the labels muted and the values in `--foreground`, linking where they can: "Built by 05 · 09", "Implements §01, §03". The heading is a size container (`container-type: inline-size`), so the bar and the card follow the column's width, not the viewport's. Nothing in the heading is sticky; it scrolls with the text.

The frontier hints (`.trace-frontier`, `trace/frontier-hints.tsx`) are at most five link lines at the head of the spine, one line each at `--type-ui` in `--muted-foreground` with an end ellipsis at the column's edge: offers to follow, quieter than the group rows below them. Edge chips (`.trace-chips`, `trace/edge-chips.tsx`) follow a summary sentence as mono `--type-fine` pills on `--muted`, at most 28ch each, their text in `--foreground` (the settle word too), resting at 70% opacity (Ledger needs about 63% for 4.5:1 text contrast) and whole while their sentence is hovered or holds focus, while a chip has focus, and inside an opened run; they are hidden by opacity, never `display`, so they keep their place in the accessibility tree and the tab order. The turn footer's impact rows (`.trace-impact`) sit under a changed file's chip behind a hairline on their start edge, indented to the chip's words, one `--type-ui` row per document.

## Loading, empty and error states

A container waiting on data, holding nothing, or failing to load draws `LoadState` (`ui/load-state.tsx`, `load-state.css`) and nothing else: a glyph, a title at `--type-body`/500 in `--foreground`, an optional detail at `--type-ui` in `--muted-foreground` (drawn through `Untrusted`), and an optional action row (one secondary button, then links). It is centred on both axes in whatever holds it: it grows into a flex column's spare height and, as an only child, takes its parent's full height, so the parent must have a definite height (the shell's `main`; `.plan-layout`, a grid so its page area stretches; `.changes-page` and `.concept-unloaded` at `min-height: 100%`; the file mode columns at `min-h-full`). `size="compact"` tightens the padding for a popover, a card, a dialog section or a sidebar.

- Loading is the spinner (`ui/spinner.tsx`): a hairline ring at 18% of `currentColor` and one round-capped arc that turns (900ms) while its length breathes, at the state's icon size (40px on a page, 24px compact) on the page's own background. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not turn and the arc's opacity eases instead. The state is `role="status"` named by its title (a muted 400), and fades in after 160ms so a load that answers at once never flashes.
- Empty (`Inbox`) and not found (`SearchX`) are the state's figure: a 40px muted icon at stroke 1.25 in a 72px `--muted` disc on a page, 24px in a 44px disc when compact; error (`CircleAlert`) takes `--tone-blocked` on the icon alone. An inline-glyph size (14 to 20px) is never a state's figure. No dashed box, no hazard stripes, no card.
- A busy line inside a control or after an action ("Sending…", "Uploading…") is `BusyText` (`ui/busy-text.tsx`): the 1em arrow spinner before the words (Status, rule 2), inside the caller's own role. A failed action's message is `ActionError` (`ui/action-error.tsx`), an alert line under the control, never `LoadState`.
- A sentence inside a list, a shelf or a partial page (an empty shelf's explanation, "No open questions", a concept page's empty-state note above its offers) stays `.plan-empty`: one muted sentence where the content would start. The home and kickoff doodles are the only illustrations.

## Focus and hover

- Focus: `outline: 2px solid var(--ring)` at 2px offset on every focusable thing, the same as the reading column, including the Settings theme cards (`components/theme-picker.tsx`, portalled outside `[data-loom-plan]`). No ring shadows, no inner glows. One sign per focused thing: a box around words (the chat composer, the kickoff field) draws the ring off its resting edge while the words have focus and never also turns its edge to `--ring`, which drew a second ring inside the first; a wrapper draws a `:focus-within` ring only over a control that draws none of its own (the pressure card's hidden select, the help search). `ui/focus-rings.test.ts` scans every planner sheet for an edge turned to the ring beside an outline or under `:focus-within`. The one displaced ring is the feature picker's (New work): a listbox shows its focus on its active option, inset.
- Hover: `--muted` wash on quiet and secondary buttons and on rows that open; the primary darkens through `color-mix` with its own foreground. Transitions are 120ms and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- A link in chrome underlines on hover only; a link in reading prose is always underlined.

## Do not

Drawn from the screens before this system:

- Do not put a bordered button inside a bordered card, or a card inside a card grid's card.
- Do not repeat the banner's text under a blocked button (home's second `loom plan is in read-only mode` line): the reason line is enough.
- Do not make a label a sentence (`Move into a feature…`).
- Do not fill a button with `--primary` unless it is the screen's one primary.
- Do not tint a surface with the accent, or draw a gradient behind a card.
- Do not draw a six-segment coloured progress bar on a card; write the counts (Home's strip of step marks, under Surfaces, is glyphs beside a written count).
- Do not set two mono paths in a row under a title; one, truncated, is enough.
- Do not set a banner in two lines of toned text; one line, glyph toned, text in `--foreground`.
- Do not hatch or stripe a panel to show read-only; dim the control and state the reason.
- Do not cast a shadow on an in-page card or restyle a button from a wrapper (`[&_button]:`, `.x button`); pass `look`.
