# Planner visual system

Rules for every planner surface. The scales and primitives are in `design.css` (imported once from `plan.css`); the tone comes from the reading column on the product page: type and space do the work, and the chrome stays out of the way of the text. A rule that fails in one of the four themes (Ledger, Aubergine, Walnut, Graphite) fails here.

## Colour

- Theme tokens only: `--background`, `--card`, `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--muted-foreground`, `--border`, `--hairline`, `--primary`, `--primary-foreground`, `--ring`, `--accent-ink`, `--accent-text`, `--tone-*`, `--stat-*` (the four token components of Settings' statistics, below), `--doc-1..8`. No colour literal, no `color-mix` that invents a hue, no gradient.
- The themes (`tokens.css`): Ledger, light, stone paper with the amber `--primary` `oklch(0.84 0.155 80)`; Walnut, dark (offered in the dashboard and the planner), a warm brown-black at hue 55 (`.dark`); Graphite, the dark default (`theme-default.ts` seeds it), a neutral charcoal at hue 270 (`.dark[data-dark-theme="gray"]`); Aubergine, a purple dark at hue 325 (`.dark[data-dark-theme="aubergine"]`), is offered in the planner but never its default. Dark surfaces (`--background`, `--card`, `--muted`, `--secondary`, `--accent`, `--border`, the sidebar tokens) keep chroma at or under 0.015, so the only saturated colours on a page are the accent and the status tones. Measured (WCAG relative luminance from OKLCH): Walnut's foreground on its background 14.6:1, muted foreground on its card 6.7:1, the orange on its background 7.7:1 and the background on the orange 7.7:1; Graphite 14.7:1, 6.6:1, 7.5:1 and 7.5:1.
- The dark accent is orange, `oklch(0.74 0.15 55)`, for `--primary` and `--ring`, with `--primary-foreground` the theme's own background. The accent marks the primary button's fill, the focus ring, the selection and the arrival flash in the reading column, and the chosen option of a segmented choice, and nothing else. It is never a border, a tint behind a surface, a heading colour or a status.
- Contrast (WCAG relative luminance from the OKLCH tokens): text meets 4.5:1 (1.4.3) and a control's edge, a focus ring or a glyph that carries meaning meets 3:1 (1.4.11) against what it sits on, in every theme. `--accent-ink` (`index.css`: `--ring` on Ledger, `--primary` in the dark themes) is the token for accent-coloured lines and glyphs (the specimen's rule, the chosen size tile's edge and glyph, the live wrap-up dot), 3.5:1 on the Ledger page and 3.8:1 on its card, where the amber fill is 1.5:1. `--accent-text` (`index.css`: a deeper ochre `oklch(0.5 0.11 80)` on Ledger, `--primary` in the dark themes) is the token for an accent-coloured text run (New work's eyebrow, a pending tool row's target), 4.9:1 on the Ledger page, 5.3:1 on its card and 4.6:1 on `--muted`; in the dark themes both are the orange at 7.5:1 or more.
- In the dark themes the warning tones (`--tone-warning`, `--hazard-warning`) are amber at hue 80, 25 degrees off the orange accent at hue 55, so a warning never reads as the accent; Ledger's warning stays orange (hue 50) beside its amber accent. Every dark tone measures at least 3.4:1 on the Walnut and the Graphite card (`--tone-completed` 8.8:1 and 8.6:1), and the Ledger tones at least 3.4:1 on the Ledger card. As every status, a warning is a glyph and a word, never a hue alone.
- `--tone-*` colours a glyph, never a text run, a border or a background. Warnings use `--tone-warning`; `--destructive` is for an action that deletes.

## Type

Chrome is Alegreya Sans (`--font-sans`); reading prose keeps `--font-reading`; paths, ids and code keep `--font-mono`. Sentence case everywhere; the `eyebrow` utility is the only uppercase.

| Token          | Size                            | Use                                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `--type-page`  | 20px/650, `.plan-page-title`    | the one title of a screen, under its eyebrow                                                                        |
| `--type-title` | 15px/600, `.plan-section-title` | a section inside a page, a panel head                                                                               |
| `--type-body`  | 14px/400                        | chrome prose: a sentence beside a button, a note, an empty state; the spine's rows and the chat's rows and controls |
| `--type-ui`    | 13px/500                        | buttons, rows, tabs, labels; in the spine and the chat, what is `.plan-fine` elsewhere                              |
| `--type-fine`  | 12px/400, `.plan-fine`          | a reason under a control, a count, a timestamp                                                                      |

The document step: a feature document's pages (its summary and source headings, Coverage and Findings) are read as documents, so their headings sit larger than `--type-page`, all in the chrome face (`--font-sans`) at 600 with -0.02em tracking. Alegreya Sans is a text face, drawn at 119% (`size-adjust`, `fonts.css`), and its default spacing opens up at these sizes, so -0.02em closes each heading into one word shape; it has no 600, so the 700 file serves 600 and the headings draw in its bold, a clear step above the 500 rows under them. The page title is 40px on a 46px line (30px on a 36px line under 30rem of heading width, `trace/trace-heading.css`; on Coverage and Findings 32px under a 480px viewport, `trace/maps.css`); a section header (a Coverage section, a Findings group) 24px; a finding title 22px (20px under 36rem of list width), a plan page's section heading and the settle confirmation's title 22px. Row headings under them are `--font-sans` at 500: a Coverage heading row 18px, a What it implements row 17px, a decided finding's title 16px; the finding titles in an open Coverage row are 17px at 600. The scope standfirst under the title is the reading face at 1.1 × `--reading-size`; the record, quote and point text on those pages is the reading face at 0.9 × `--reading-size`. No title or heading uses the reading face. Every other screen keeps `--type-page`.

The two sidebars sit one step up the scale from the rest of the chrome, because they are read beside the reading column, not glanced at. In the spine (`feature/spine.css`): rows and the group names at `--type-body`; the mono ids and numbers, the counts, a section's state word and the "Source" line at `--type-ui`; every row leads with one 14px glyph (Status); the head's next-step sentence is the reading face at 0.9 × `--reading-size` and its button 32px at `--type-body`. In the chat panel (`chat.css` root is `--type-body`): chrome (tool rows and fold line, notices, chip labels, context row, queue rows, permission band) at `--type-body`; the head title at `--type-title` (600; "New chat" at 500 in `--muted-foreground`) over its sub-line, the state word in `--foreground` and the place in `--muted-foreground`, at `--type-ui`; the empty state's title at 26px/500 and the left-behind title at 20px/500, both in `--font-reading`; user turns and the composer field at 15px (`--type-title`, turns at 500, field at 400); the assistant's prose at 16px/1.55 in `--font-reading`; secondary text (chip source, file size, timestamps, counts, `.plan-fine`, `size="sm"` buttons) and mono tool and permission bodies at `--type-ui`; the composer's foot line at `--type-fine`, the panel's one 12px text (Chat). The kickoff transcript inherits the chat's sizes. Full-size buttons in the chat are 14px; the hearth's shaped buttons (Chat) are 32px. Nothing else in either sidebar drops below 13px.

Hierarchy is made with size, weight and space, in that order. A surface uses the weights the scale above names (400, 500, 600, and 650 for the page title) and no others. Muted text (`--muted-foreground`) marks what is secondary; it never marks what is disabled.

## Space

`--space-1..7` = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48px. Inside a control and between a glyph and its word: 1-2. Between rows: 2-3. Between a title and its content: 3. Between sections: 5-6, with no rule and no box between them. Page gutter: `--page-gutter` (24px, 16px below 480px). Reading text keeps its 68ch column; chrome prose stops at 48ch.

## Surfaces and borders

- The page ground is `--background`. One level of surface exists: `.plan-surface` (`--card`, hairline edge, 10px corners) standing on the ground. Nothing inside a surface draws a border, a shadow or another surface. A box in a box is not allowed.
- A border is allowed around: an input, a surface standing alone on the page, a popover or dialog. A border is not allowed around: a button row, a list that sits on the page, a status, a heading, a notice.
- Lists divide with hairlines (`.plan-list`, `.plan-rule`); their edges are the page's edges. A hairline separates; it never encloses.
- Shadows exist only on things that float: popovers and dialogs (`--plan-shadow`, `--dialog-shadow`; the activity popover layers `--popover-lift`, a longer drop, under `--plan-shadow`). Nothing in the page flow casts one.
- An open block in the reading column (a sentence opened to its source passages) draws a 2px edge down its start in its document's hue (`--doc-n`, through `--run-colour`). It is the one coloured border on the page: the hue says which document, never a status.

## Buttons

`DisabledReason` draws every button (`.plan-button`, 28px high, 13px/500, 6px corners, one line, `white-space: nowrap`). `OfferButton` wraps it for an offer. Three looks through `look`:

- `quiet`: no edge, muted text until hovered. Toolbar actions, Source and Edit, icon buttons.
- exception: the header's Chat button (`ChatSheetButton`, `chat/panel-collapse.tsx`) is a quiet `.plan-button` that is always shown on a chat route. From 1280px it is the side panel's disclosure: `aria-expanded` while the panel is open fills it `--muted` in `--foreground` (the `.plan-button` expanded state), and it is named "Collapse chat" or "Expand chat" with the state and counts. Below 1280px it opens the sheet. Its decision count is a 16px `--primary` pill and its attachment count a 16px `--muted` pill with a `--border` ring, both inside the 28px line box.
- `secondary`: hairline edge, transparent. Every offer and action by default.
- `primary`: `--primary` fill. At most one per screen, and only its owner passes `look="primary"`: the surface that draws `checklist.primary` (the pace card holding it on `product` and `engineering` while pace shows, the queue row, or the page that owns it), a dialog's confirm (a dialog floats and owns its own), and the Start of the home and kickoff screens. `DisabledReason` is secondary unless told otherwise. The chat's own primaries, one per surface, are under Chat.

`shape` (`pill` or `round`, drawn as `data-shape`, `ui/hearth.css`) is the hearth's 32px full-radius variant, used only in the chat and on `/new` (Chat); it wins over `size="sm"`. `reasonId` names an element the caller draws the blocked reason in; the button is described by it and draws none of its own, so the reason is still drawn once.

`size="sm"` is the 24px/12px row variant for an action inside a list row, a panel head or the document heading (Page rhythm), where every control (Edit, Discuss and Open all in the toolbar bar, the Summary / Source switch's segments, Rebuild and Pressure-test at a status row's end) is a bare `.plan-button` of that size, quiet, on one 24px centre line; Pressure-test alone carries its flask and a resting `--muted` fill (a `--border` edge on a summary page's chip row), because beside a status word a bare quiet button reads as one more word. The heading draws no secondary and no primary button: the bar and the status card carry the edges, and the quiet buttons sit inside them. A feature document's summary page is the exception (Surfaces): its action row is 32px bordered buttons. A label is a verb plus at most two words (`Start`, `Move`, `Rebuild`), except that an offer's server label is drawn as sent (`Write plan feat-planner-01-documents`); a label carrying a document's slug truncates and never wraps. The sentence beside the button explains, so the button never carries the explanation. A screen never draws one offer twice: each offer has one owner, decided by its id.

Blocked: `aria-disabled`, 50% opacity, no hover change, and the reason beneath in `.plan-fine`, or after the button on its line in the document header's summary status line (`reasonPlacement="inline"`). The reason is the control's accessible description, so it is drawn once per blocked control and never repeated in a second line or a banner under the same control.

## Status

Status is a glyph and a word (`.plan-status`), inline with the text it describes: `✓ done`, `○ not written`, `· optional`, and the current item marked with `--primary`. The word keeps the text's colour; only the glyph takes a tone. No coloured chips, no pill backgrounds, no segmented progress bars, no coloured left borders for a status (an open block's edge, under Surfaces, names a document). A count reads as text (`12/12`, `3 plans`) in `.plan-fine`. An out-of-date item is dashed, not recoloured. The document key writes `changed` in `.plan-fine` beside a document that changed since the reader looked.

Five signs, one per job, and none stands in for another:

1. **The roundel** (`BusyRoundel`, `@/aurora-ui/feedback/BusyRoundel`), the black-and-white disc, is drawn in two places and nowhere else. The header notification pill (`header/activity.tsx`, `PillGlyph`) is the planner's global activity indicator, turning while any work runs, inside an `aria-hidden` wrapper sized by `.activity-glyph` (the disc at 86% of the box, the reach of a lucide glyph's strokes); when the most urgent activity is your turn, the pill draws that kind's bell or LED instead. A running plan's row in the spine (`feature/spine-row.tsx`, `.plan-spine__glyph[data-glyph="running"]`) leads with the same disc at the pill's size, about 13px in its 14px glyph box, in place of the status dot: the row is the work the pill counts.

## Loading, empty and error states

A container waiting on data, holding nothing, or failing to load draws `LoadState` (`ui/load-state.tsx`, `load-state.css`) and nothing else: a glyph, a title at `--type-body`/500 in `--foreground`, an optional detail at `--type-ui` in `--muted-foreground` (drawn through `Untrusted`), and an optional action row (one secondary button, then links). It is centred on both axes in whatever holds it: it grows into a flex column's spare height and, as an only child, takes its parent's full height, so the parent must have a definite height (the shell's `main`; `.plan-layout`, a grid so its page area stretches; `.changes-page` and `.concept-unloaded` at `min-height: 100%`; the file mode columns at `min-h-full`). `size="compact"` tightens the padding for a popover, a card, a dialog section or a sidebar.

- Loading is the spinner (`ui/spinner.tsx`): a hairline ring at 18% of `currentColor` and one round-capped arc that turns (900ms) while its length breathes, at the state's icon size (40px on a page, 24px compact) on the page's own background. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it does not turn and the arc's opacity eases instead. The state is `role="status"` named by its title (a muted 400), and fades in after 160ms so a load that answers at once never flashes.
- Empty (`Inbox`) and not found (`SearchX`) are the state's figure: a 40px muted icon at stroke 1.25 in a 72px `--muted` disc on a page, 24px in a 44px disc when compact; error (`CircleAlert`) takes `--tone-blocked` on the icon alone. An inline-glyph size (14 to 20px) is never a state's figure. No dashed box, no hazard stripes, no card.
- A busy line inside a control or after an action ("Sending…", "Uploading…") is `BusyText` (`ui/busy-text.tsx`): the 1em arrow spinner before the words (Status, rule 2), inside the caller's own role. A failed action's message is `ActionError` (`ui/action-error.tsx`), an alert line under the control, never `LoadState`.
- A sentence inside a list, a shelf or a partial page (an empty shelf's explanation, "No open questions", a concept page's empty-state note above its offers) stays `.plan-empty`: one muted sentence where the content would start. The home and kickoff doodles are the only illustrations.

## Focus and hover

- Focus: `outline: 2px solid var(--ring)` at 2px offset on every focusable thing, the same as the reading column, including the Settings theme cards (`components/theme-picker.tsx`, portalled outside `[data-loom-plan]`). No ring shadows, no inner glows. One sign per focused thing: a box around words (the chat composer, the kickoff field) draws the ring off its resting edge while the words have focus and never also turns its edge to `--ring`, which drew a second ring inside the first; a wrapper draws a `:focus-within` ring only over a control that draws none of its own (the pressure card's hidden select, the help search). `ui/focus-rings.test.ts` scans every planner sheet for an edge turned to the ring beside an outline or under `:focus-within`. The displaced rings: the feature picker's (New work), where a listbox shows its focus on its active option, inset; a Coverage tile's, inset because the tile band clips its corners (`trace/maps.css`); a settle menu item's, inset because the menu scrolls (`trace/trace-settle.css`); and a Coverage row's, drawn round the whole line by its toggle's `::after`.
- Hover: `--muted` wash on quiet and secondary buttons and on rows that open; the primary darkens through `color-mix` with its own foreground. Transitions are 120ms and off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- A link in chrome underlines on hover only; a link in reading prose is always underlined.

## Do not

Drawn from the screens before this system:

- Do not put a bordered button inside a bordered card, or a card inside a card grid's card.
- Do not repeat the banner's text under a blocked button (home's second `loom plan is in read-only mode` line): the reason line is enough.
- Do not make a label a sentence (`Move into a feature…`).
- Do not fill a button with `--primary` unless it is the screen's one primary.
- Do not tint a surface with the accent, or draw a gradient behind a card.
- Do not draw a six-segment coloured progress bar on a card; write the counts (Home's strip of step marks, under Surfaces, is glyphs beside a written count).
- Do not set two mono paths in a row under a title; one, truncated, is enough.
- Do not set a banner in two lines of toned text; one line, glyph toned, text in `--foreground`.
- Do not hatch or stripe a panel to show read-only; dim the control and state the reason.
- Do not cast a shadow on an in-page card or restyle a button from a wrapper (`[&_button]:`, `.x button`); pass `look`.
